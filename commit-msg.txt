Write the single-branch fold once, in Ladder.lean

red_fold_one. When a ladder has trMax 1, a zero head in both rows, and brF of
length one, red's first branch cuts [(0,0),(1,1)] and recurses on the branch's
reconstruction:

    red (f+1) M = jjSeq 0 1 ++ incrFirst (red f ((jnJ+1, nJ+1) :: derp B)) (jnJ - nJ)

Stated over an arbitrary ladder with the five readings as hypotheses, so a phase
supplies its trMax, brF, firstNodes, joints and nJ and gets the level. That is the
last piece of machinery link 2 needs; what remains is supplying it five times.

Axioms: [propext, Quot.sound].
