Measured before routing the general Veblen certificate through

    cnvAll       215 distinct CNV terms   NfOK fails on 34

    Evidence.WF.NfOK Evidence.WF.rowA  =  FALSE
    rowA = phi(0, phi(phi(0,0),0)) = φ̄(0, ε₀) = ω^(ε₀+1)

**`rowA` IS AN ALREADY-REGISTERED, ALREADY-CERTIFIED ✅ ROW.**  It is the eleventh
entry of `Evidence.Cert.certRows`, and its certificate does not go through the
assembly at all.  Every other registered row is `NfOK`; `rowA` is the one that is
not:

    0, 1, 2, ω, ω·2, ω², ω^ω, ω^(ω^ω), ε₀, ε₀+1        NfOK = true
    ω^(ε₀+1)                                            NfOK = FALSE

WHAT THIS MEANS
---------------
`NfOK` is the recursion-local normality predicate built (WF §15.38) so that
`asm_generalB'` would not be vacuous.  It encodes `phiNF a b = φ̄(a,b)` at every
`φ̄` node the recursion visits.

`φ̄(0, ε₀)` is **exactly the fixed-point-skip term**: `φ̄` enumerates the additively
principal numbers SKIPPING the fixed points of `ω^·`, so `φ̄(0,ε₀) = ω^(ε₀+1) > ε₀`,
while `phiNF 0 ε₀ = φ̄(1,0) = ε₀`.  It is the same term that refuted the assembly's
old global `Hnf` (WF §15.31, §15.37).

So the situation is sharper than "the assembly carries a side condition":

* **`NfOK` is NOT NECESSARY for certification.**  `rowA` is certified without it.
  The condition is an artifact of core (C)'s `hside`, not a property of certifiable
  rows.
* **`asm_generalB'`'s reach is strictly smaller than the Veblen band** — 34 of 169
  corpus terms, 20%, are outside it, and at least one of them is a published ✅.
* Therefore **the general certificate `∀ t, CNV t → Certified (sqv' t) t` cannot be
  routed entirely through `asm_generalB'`.**  The `φ̄(a, ·)`-at-a-fixed-point cases
  need a different supplier for their fundamental sequence.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
-------------------
That the general certificate is impossible.  `Certified.lim` takes `fs'` as a
PARAMETER; the assembly is one way to produce it, not the only way.  `rowA`'s own
certificate is the existence proof that the excluded cases are reachable by other
means.
